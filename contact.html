Good to Know

Frequently asked, honestly answered

Our plating is measured in microns — 1 micron on silver vermeil, 2 on brass — versus the 0.03-micron “gold flash” most brands use. With normal wear (yes, including showers we don’t officially recommend), pieces keep their finish 12–24 months, and can be re-plated at the atelier for a nominal ₹299. A soft patina is normal; a polish cloth restores shine.

Polki is uncut, flat-backed diamond — the look of temple-era India. Natural polki costs as much as a small car. Moissanite polki gives you the same uncut sparkle and flat-back setting at a tenth of the price, without the mining. We say “moissanite” on every listing because we believe you should always know what you’re wearing.

Yes — all posts are hypoallergenic (sterling silver or surgical steel), and nothing we make contains nickel or lead. Sensitive-ear customers are among our most repeated buyers. If a piece ever irritates you, exchange it within 7 days, no questions asked.

All orders are prepaid — UPI, cards or net-banking. UPI orders get 5% off automatically — because you’re trusting us first, and we think that’s worth something.
