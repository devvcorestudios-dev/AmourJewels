Our Story

From our atelier, with love.

Amour began in 2019 with two workbenches, four karigars and a stubborn belief — that the jewellery India loves for weddings shouldn’t spend the rest of the year locked in a bank locker.

Today, every piece is still hand-finished in our atelier: 92.5 silver and brass, plated in 1-micron 18k gold, set with moissanite polki and hand-picked pearls. Same karigars. Same standards. Just a lot more workbenches.

— Team Amour
