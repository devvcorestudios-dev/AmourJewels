Policies & Care

No fine print designed to tire you. Everything about shipping, exchanges, care and privacy — in plain language.

Shipping Cancellation Return & Refund Exchange Warranty Care Privacy Terms Grievance Officer

01Shipping

Every order is dispatched from our Jaipur atelier within 24–48 working hours, packed in the signature keepsake box with a care card.

  • Metro cities: 2–3 working days · Rest of India: 3–6 working days, via Delhivery / BlueDart.
  • Shipping is free on orders above ₹2,500; below that, a flat ₹99.
  • Every shipment is fully insured — in the rare event of loss or damage in transit, we replace the piece, not argue about it.
  • COD is available up to ₹5,000. Orders above ₹5,000 must be prepaid — UPI, cards or net-banking. UPI prepaid orders get 5% off, applied automatically.
  • International: DHL Express to 40+ countries, 4–8 working days. Duties at destination. WhatsApp us for a quote.

02Cancellation

Before dispatch: cancel free of charge any time before your order ships — from Track Order, or over WhatsApp. Prepaid refunds reflect in 3–5 working days to the original payment method.

After dispatch: orders already with the courier can't be cancelled; refuse delivery and the shipment returns to us (RTO), after which we refund prepaid orders minus actual two-way shipping.

Partial cancellations: multi-item orders can be cancelled item-wise before dispatch; the rest ships as usual.

03Return & Refund

We want you to love it — and we'd rather take it back than have you keep something you don't.

  • 7-day return window from delivery. Piece must be unworn, in its box, with tags intact.
  • Refunds to the original payment method in 3–5 working days after quality check; COD orders are refunded by UPI or bank transfer.
  • Earrings are returnable only for manufacturing defects — for hygiene reasons we hope you understand.
  • Defect on arrival? Photograph it within 48 hrs of delivery and we'll courier a replacement before the week is out.
  • Custom and engraved pieces are made for you alone — refundable only for defects.

04Exchange

  • 7-day size exchange, no questions asked — we arrange a reverse pickup and ship your new size free.
  • Ring resizing: free within 30 days, then a nominal ₹499.
  • Exchange for a different design: pay or get refunded the difference, one exchange per order.
  • Chain length and dori adjustments are free, forever, at the atelier or by post.

05Warranty

  • 6-month manufacturing warranty on plating and stone-setting from delivery date.
  • Covers: plating defects, stone loss under normal wear, clasp or dori failure.
  • Doesn't cover: water/chemical damage, bent or snapped items from impact, lost pieces.
  • Claim: WhatsApp photos + order ID — repairs or replacement in 7–10 days.
  • Plating refresh (re-polish + re-plate): ₹299 per piece, lifetime, for pieces outside warranty too.

06Jewellery Care

Vermeil is real gold over real silver — treat it like the quiet luxury it is:

  • Last on, first off. Perfume, lotion and hairspray before jewellery; jewellery off before shower, gym and swim.
  • Wipe gently with the flannel pouch after wear; store pieces flat and apart so stones don’t scratch stones.
  • A soft patina is natural on silver and vermeil. A gold-tone polish cloth revives shine in seconds.
  • Pearls are porous — never spray them, never soak them. Just wipe, pouch, done.
  • Plating refresh (re-polish + re-plate): ₹299 per piece at the atelier, lifetime. Because we mean the “forever” part.

07Privacy

We collect only what an order needs: your name, contact and address. We don’t sell, rent or “share with 212 trusted partners” — your data goes to the courier and to nobody else. Newsletter consent is separate, one click to leave, and we don’t chase you across the internet afterwards. Payment details never touch our servers; they’re handled by our PCI-DSS payment gateway.

08Terms of Sale

All prices are in INR and inclusive of GST. Product photography is of the actual designs, but natural stones and pearls vary by nature — consider it a feature, not a flaw. Slight size variation (±5%) is normal for hand-finished work. By ordering, you confirm you’re 18+ or shopping with a guardian’s blessing. Disputes, if ever, settle in the courts of Jaipur, Rajasthan — where everything we make begins.
