Home  /  Our Story

Made in Jaipur. Worn everywhere.

A demi-fine jewellery atelier built on one stubborn idea — the pieces India saves for weddings deserve better than a bank locker the other 360 days.

“Our grandmother’s jewellery was real because it was kept. Ours is real because it is worn — to the office, the airport, the third wedding of the season.”
— The Amour Atelier, Johari Bazaar
How It Started

Two workbenches, four karigars, one “why not us”.

In 2019, we watched a friend pay more for flight tickets than her jewellery budget — then leave the jewellery home because it was “too precious”. That weekend, we set up a workshop above a Polki trader in Johari Bazaar.

The rule was simple from day one: honest materials, hand-set stones, plating measured in microns — not marketing. No mass casting. No “gold-toned alloy”. If we wouldn’t gift it to our own sisters, it doesn’t ship.

Same rule today. Just 40 more pairs of hands.

What We Stand By

The Amour standard

Honest materials

92.5 sterling silver and brass only — never mystery alloy. Plating measured in microns, disclosed on every product page.

Hand-set stones

Moissanite polki, zircon and pearls set stone-by-stone by karigars we know by name — most with us since the first year.

Small batches

Drops of 40–200 pieces. When a design sells out, it usually stays sold — we’d rather cut a new stone than over-run a mould.

Fair karigar wages

Above-market piece rates, festival bonuses and health cover for our 40+ craftspeople. Craft survives when the craftsman does.

The Journey So Far

Seven years, four workshops

2019 · Jaipur

The first workbench

Two founders, four karigars and a rented loft in Johari Bazaar. First collection: twelve designs. First customer: a bride from Bikaner who still DMs us every Diwali.

2021 · The leap

Instagram becomes our storefront

Our polki-first reels travel. The waitlist crosses 4,000. We move to a proper atelier and bring plating in-house, so nothing leaves without a micron check.

2023 · The standard

Honest materials, on paper

We publish base metal, plating micronage and stone type on every product page — rare among demi-fine brands, non-negotiable since.

2026 · Today

68,000 orders later

Forty-plus karigars, a bridal studio, and pieces worn from sangeets in Ludhiana to signings in Manhattan. Still handmade. Still Jaipur.
